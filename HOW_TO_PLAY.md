# How to play Mission Control

A cooperative game for **2 to 5 players** (4 crew + 1 Commander). Empty crew seats are flown by AutoCrew bots.
A mission takes about **10 to 15 minutes**. Start with **Bagyo Watch** on **Cadet**.

## The idea
The **crew** (four laptops) builds and flies the spacecraft, but can't see whether their choices are right.
The **Commander** (the host laptop) sees the budget, the ship's health and the handbook, but can't see the crew's
gauges or clock. **Talk to each other.** That is the whole game.

Keep the Commander's screen private (don't project it).

## The seats

| Seat | You pick | In a crisis you watch |
|---|---|---|
| **FLIGHT** (Flight Director) | the orbit, the engines, the pointing | particle counter, tracking radar |
| **SYSTEMS** (Spacecraft Engineer) | the body, solar wings, battery, computer, shielding | battery voltage, computer health |
| **PAYLOAD** (Science Lead) | the instruments (cameras, radars, sensors) | detector temperature, recorder fill |
| **GROUND** (Operations and Budget) | the rocket, the antenna, the ground stations | radio signal, mission bank account |
| **COMMANDER** | nothing: you check everyone's picks and find the crises | the handbook |

Each crew member also gets a **secret goal** worth teamwork points. Tap **MY JOB** any time for a reminder.

## A mission, step by step

### 1. Briefing
Read the mission, its goals and this game's **twist**. Press **I'M READY!**

### 2. Build (the clock is running)
- Each crew screen shows **one decision at a time**. Use **< >** at the top to change page and the big arrows to see
  more cards. Tap **Pick** (or **Add** for instruments and stations) and **?** to learn more.
- The right side shows **MISSION GOALS** (how full each goal is with our instruments, in our orbit) and the
  **TEAM SHIP** (everyone's picks). Plan together: FLIGHT's orbit decides how well each goal can be seen, and
  PAYLOAD's instruments fill the goals.
- Tell the Commander what you picked. The Commander taps a part to flag it **SWAP**; you'll see it on your card.
- The Commander's screen says exactly what to fix first ("Tell GROUND: add the Perth station") and gives a science forecast.
- When the clock runs out, investors start taking money back, and FLIGHT loses time tokens. Don't dawdle!
- Ready? Anyone presses **CALL DESIGN REVIEW**.

### 3. Launch vote
Every crew member answers **GO** or **NO-GO**, and so does the Commander. Any NO-GO sends everyone back to fix the
ship. Nobody launches while a part is flagged SWAP.

### 4. Launch
Countdown! The rocket's reliability decides whether it reaches orbit (on Cadet it always does).

### 5. Flight
The ship flies through a few events:
- **ANOMALY (crisis).** Crew: read your two gauges out loud ("Particle counter: ALARM!"). Commander: tap the alarms
  into the Gauge Finder (or use the handbook's crisis finder) and tell each crew member which card to play.
  Crew: play it, then tap **I'm done**. If your screen flashes red, the Commander is running out of time.
- **Lucky chance (opportunity).** One crew member taps **CHIP IN** and finishes a quick mini-task. Done = the team
  wins extra science, and you earn a token.
- **Surprise maintenance.** Sometimes everyone gets a quick mini-task at once.
- **Decision.** A choice that costs tokens and can earn points.

### 6. Debrief
Your score, grade, medals, who used your data, what your mission is most like in real life, why your science was
what it was, and a mission patch. Press **Again!** to fly again with the same crew.

## Tokens
When the ship launches, its **spare** power, fuel, money, data, signal and time become **tokens** (up to 3 of each,
plus bonuses). Crisis cards cost tokens. Some cards need a **teammate's** token: tap **ASK**, and they tap **GIVE**.
More spare room when you build = more tokens when it counts.

## Lives
| Cadet | Engineer | Flight Director |
|---|---|---|
| 3 lives | 2 lives | 1 life |

A crisis is a **mistake** when the crew blocks **less than half** of it, or someone plays a card that **doesn't help**.
Each mistake costs a life. No lives left = the mission is called off. Only play the cards the Commander names!

## Mini-tasks
| Seat | Task |
|---|---|
| FLIGHT | **Asteroid field:** tap the space rocks before they hit the ship. |
| SYSTEMS | **Fix the wiring:** drag each wire to the socket with the same colour and shape. |
| PAYLOAD | **Decode the data:** read a binary number (add the numbers under every 1). |
| GROUND | **Align the antenna:** drag the signal through the maze; touching a wall starts you over. |

Every finished task gives you 1 token of your own kind (GROUND gets money).

## Mission twists
Each game rolls one surprise rule (the host can switch twists off): *Solar maximum*, *Tight funding*,
*Generous sponsor*, *Busy sky*, *Rushed operations*, *Spare parts shelf*, *Maintenance week*, *Lucky paw*.

## Difficulty
| | Cadet | Engineer | Flight Director |
|---|---|---|---|
| Budget | +25% | normal | -10% |
| Crises | softer, 50% more time | normal | harsher, **Radio Silence** (crew may not talk during crises; use the Ping Wheel) |
| Launch can fail | no | yes | yes |
| Lives | 3 | 2 | 1 |

## Scoring (out of 1000)
Science 400, lifetime 150, budget 150, safety margins 100, crisis handling 150, teamwork (secret goals) 50.
**S** 850+, **A** 700+, **B** 550+, **C** 400+, **D** below.

**Why is our science low?** One instrument rarely fills a goal: use two or three on the same goal. The orbit
decides how well each goal is seen. Then everything is multiplied by power (enough?), data (does it all get home?)
and lifetime (does the ship last?). Clouds hide the ground from cameras, cameras need sunlight, and sharp
instruments need steady pointing. The handbook's *Science* page has the full table.

## Medals
| Medal | How |
|---|---|
| Frugal Flyer | spend 3/4 of the money or less |
| Margin Master | keep every safety margin full |
| Great Minds | fly 2 instruments that work great together |
| Perfect Pass | beat every crisis with no damage |
| Silent Running | Perfect Pass on Flight Director |
| Local Hero | Bagyo Watch with a tropical orbit and rain gauges |
| Helping Paws | hand 2 tokens to teammates |

## The missions
| Mission | Story | It teaches |
|---|---|---|
| **Bagyo Watch** (start here) | a typhoon and flood satellite for the Philippines | clouds vs radar, power, orbit coverage |
| **Moon Relay** | a relay and ice mapper for the lunar south pole | orbits that keep Earth in view, fuel, mass |
| **Deep Probe** | a Jupiter flyby or orbiter | faint sunlight, radiation, slow long-distance radio |

## Keys
| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **T** | open the crew chat |
| **Tab** | Commander: peek at the big Mission Control view |
| **N** | debrief: show the numbers |
| **F11** | full screen |
| **Esc** | host: back to the lobby |

## Tips for the Commander
- At every crisis ask: **"Is anything on ALARM?"** Gauges not listed must say OK.
- Only name the cards the handbook says. A wrong card costs a life.
- Use **HANDBOOK > Mission** to help FLIGHT and PAYLOAD pick an orbit and instruments together.
- Comms red? **HANDBOOK > Ground stations**: a station must reach the orbit and have the antenna's radio.
